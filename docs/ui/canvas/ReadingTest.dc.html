Urban green spaces

Urban green spaces provide more than attractive scenery. Studies suggest that spending time in parks can reduce stress and encourage physical activity.

In one city, a former car park was transformed into a community garden in 2019. Local residents now grow vegetables there and take part in weekend workshops. The project is managed by volunteers rather than a private company.

Although maintaining these spaces requires funding, supporters argue that the benefits to public health make the investment worthwhile.

1. What is one benefit of spending time in parks?
A. Reduced stress
B. Higher traffic levels
C. Longer working hours
2. When was the car park transformed?
A. 2009
B. 2019
C. 2023
3. Who manages the garden?
A. A private company
B. The police
C. Volunteers
4. What does flexible online learning require?
A. Self-discipline
B. Expensive equipment
C. Daily travel
