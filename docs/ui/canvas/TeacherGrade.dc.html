Đề bài: Some people think that governments should spend more money on public transport rather than building new roads. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
In recent years, the question of how best to spend public money on transport has become increasingly controversial. While some argue that new roads are necessary to reduce congestion, I strongly believe that investment in public transport is a more effective long-term solution.

Firstly, an efficient network of buses and trains can carry far more passengers than private cars, which reduce both traffic and air pollution. For example, cities such as Singapore have achieved this by making public transport cheap and reliable.
